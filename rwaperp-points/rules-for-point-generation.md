# Rules for Point Generation

RWAPerp Points are used to track users' genuine transactions, fund retention, invitations, and ecosystem contributions on the platform.

### 1. Weekly Base Point Generation

RWAPerp uses a fixed weekly point pool mechanism for general users:

1,000,000 Points / Week

The weekly base Points fixed output is 1,000,000 Points.

All eligible users will jointly participate in the allocation of the weekly point pool based on their effective contributions during that week.

The 1,000,000 Points per week do not include exclusive points and incentives for Market Makers, as Market Makers use a separate point and incentive mechanism.



### 2. How to Earn Points

Users can earn Points by making genuine and effective contributions on RWAPerp, including but not limited to:

#### Trading

* Effective Trading Volume
* Fee Contribution
* Effective Trading Days
* Quality of Holdings and OI

#### Deposit

* Effective Net Deposit
* Account Fund Balance
* Fund Retention Time

With the launch of products like Vault and LP, related fund contributions may gradually be included in the Points system.

Initially, only trading and deposit are supported. Subsequent updates to the Points system may support other dimensions, such as:

#### Referral

Inviting real users to join RWAPerp and completing an effective Deposit, Trading, or continuous use of the platform may earn corresponding Referral Points.

#### Market & Community Contribution

Participation in certain key RWA Markets, newly launched markets, and community contributions that generate real value for the RWAPerp ecosystem may also earn corresponding Points.



### 3. Limited Points Boost

During the initial Launch phase and certain designated promotional periods, RWAPerp will offer a limited-time, limited-quantity Points Boost.

Users who qualify for the promotion can receive extra point rewards in addition to the normal Base Points.

Specific details of the Boost:

* Promotional Period
* Eligibility
* Reward Amount
* Boost Rules
* Eligible Markets

will be subject to the corresponding promotional page and official announcements.

The limited-time Boost does not change RWAPerp's long-term fixed weekly base generation mechanism of 1,000,000 Base Points.



### 4. Points Settlement&#x20;

Points are statistically calculated and settled weekly.

After each Epoch ends, the system will calculate the corresponding Points based on the user's effective contribution for the week and issue them after completing data and risk review.

The following abnormal behaviors may not be counted towards points, including but not limited to:

* Self-Trading
* Wash Trading
* Cross-trading between associated accounts
* Sybil Accounts
* Abnormal volume manipulation
* Price manipulation
* Other behaviors deemed by the platform as non-genuine transactions



### Important Notes

* Market Maker Points are not included in the point pool mentioned above.
* An additional limited-time, limited-quantity Points Boost may be offered during the Launch phase and specified promotional periods.
* Points do not represent any promise of return, Token distribution promise, or guarantee of future asset value.
