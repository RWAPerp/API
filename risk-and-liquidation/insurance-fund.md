# Insurance Fund

The RWAPerp Insurance Fund covers bankrupt account debts and takes over positions that were not promptly handled by liquidators. The Insurance Fund is primarily funded through partial liquidation fees and is managed as an independent system account.

If RWAPerp provides Insurance Fund data, traders can view relevant information on the trading interface; the specific display scope and update frequency depend on actual product functionality.
