# What Is a Perpetual Future

A perpetual future ("perp") is a derivative contract that allows you to take a leveraged long or short position on an asset's price without owning the underlying asset and without an expiry date.

Unlike traditional futures contracts, a perp never settles to a delivery date. Instead, a periodic funding fee mechanism keeps the contract's price anchored to the underlying reference price.

You can go long to profit if the price rises, or short to profit if it falls, using leverage to control a position larger than your posted collateral.
