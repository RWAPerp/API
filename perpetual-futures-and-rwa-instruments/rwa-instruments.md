# RWA Instruments

RWAPerp's instruments provide price exposure to real-world assets (RWA) — specifically focusing on global equities, regional supply chain narratives, and indices — as synthetic perpetual contracts.

Important: Each contract tracks the price of its underlying asset via our Verified Market State Engine; it is not a tokenized asset and does not involve holding the underlying stock or commodity. You are trading price exposure settled in USDC, not ownership of the asset.
