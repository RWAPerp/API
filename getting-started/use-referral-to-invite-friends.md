# Use Referral to Invite Friends

Navigate to the Referral page on the left to view your exclusive:

* Referral Code
* Referral Link

<img src="../.gitbook/assets/unknown (39).png" alt="" height="379" width="785">

You can share your Referral Link to invite other users to join RWAPerp.

Currently, invited users enjoy a 5% Trading Fee Discount for their first month.

When users you directly invite start trading, you can earn corresponding Referral Rewards.

The Direct Trades rebate rate currently displayed on the page is 10%; please refer to the real-time display on the Referral page for the actual rate.

The page also summarizes your referral data, including:

* Total Volume: Total trading volume generated by invited users
* Referral Rewards: Cumulative referral rewards earned
* Referral Friends: Number of users who joined via your invitation link
* Friends Who Traded: Number of invited users who have started trading

The list below also lets you check each invited user's:

* Wallet Address
* Join Date
* Trading Volume
* Corresponding Rewards
