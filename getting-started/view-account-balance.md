# View Account Balance

After depositing, you can view your account funds on the right side of the trading interface.

<img src="../.gitbook/assets/unknown (34).png" alt="" height="334" width="698">

My Assets shows assets currently in your trading account.

Free Collateral shows available margin to support your positions.

Unrealized PnL shows the unrealized profit and loss of open positions.

Margin Ratio shows the current margin status of your account.

Currently, besides USDC, USDG is also supported in position margin calculations and can be used to open positions in markets.
