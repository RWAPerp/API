# View Market Information

Navigate to the Markets page on the left to view major indices, investment themes, and earnings calendars in one place.

<img src="../.gitbook/assets/unknown (37).png" alt="" height="292" width="602">

At the top of the page, you can check major indices such as:

S\&P 500, Nasdaq 100, Hang Seng Index, KOSPI, etc.

Under Investment Themes, you can quickly search for related assets by theme, such as:

Semiconductor, Memory, Foundry, CPU, etc.

Earnings Calendar allows you to view companies about to report or that have already reported earnings, organized by date.

Through the Markets page, you can quickly find markets, themes, and earnings events you follow.
