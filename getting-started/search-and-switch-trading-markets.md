# Search and Switch Trading Markets

You can use Search at the top of the page to search for markets currently supported by RWAPerp.

<img src="../.gitbook/assets/unknown (35).png" alt="" height="289" width="602">

RWAPerp currently covers:

* Stocks
* ETFs / Indices
* Commodities
* Crypto

After selecting a market, please confirm the ticker displayed on the current page before placing a trade.

Some stock, index, ETF, or other RWA markets may show Market closed.

This indicates that the corresponding traditional reference market is currently closed; please refer to real-time information on the page for actual trading conditions.
